How it works

Three things to do. Then read your matches.

Setup takes about five minutes and one of the three steps is just picking a city.

1

Upload your resume

A PDF is fine. We read it once and pull out your skills, your years, and — the part that matters — which of your claims you can actually back up with something you did. Anything we cannot find evidence for is marked, and never gets written onto a resume later.

2

Pick the kind of work and the cities

Tap a card — Support & Operations, SRE & Cloud, Software Development, Data, QA, Security, Networking, IT Ops — and choose your cities. That is the whole configuration. We suggest the right cards from your resume, so usually you are just confirming.

Support & Operations SRE, Cloud & DevOps Software Development Data & Analytics QA & Automation Cybersecurity Networking IT & Systems
3

Press one button

We read the career pages of employers hiring in India directly — not a job board, so nothing is stale or reposted — then score every opening that fits and hand you a ranked list with a written reason for each. From there, one tap per job gives you a rewritten resume, the messages to send, and interview prep.

What you get

Everything after "should I apply?"

A score with a reason

Out of 100, plus two sentences on what matched and what does not. A low score saves you the application; a high one tells you what to lead with.

A resume per job, that cannot lie

Reordered and rewritten for the specific posting, then checked line by line against your real history. If a bullet cannot be traced back to something you did, you are told before you send it.

The messages, written

Cover letter, a recruiter message short enough for LinkedIn, an email to the hiring manager, and the follow-up for a week later. Copy, read, send.

Interview prep for that role

The questions this posting invites, what each one is really testing, and an honest list of where you will get caught out. Plus a briefing on the company.

A salary number to ask for

A band in LPA for the role, your years and your city, and a recommended ask — with how confident we are, so you know when to go and verify it.

One board for the whole search

Every job you save moves through saved, applied, interviewing, offer. It tells you who has gone quiet and who to follow up with today.

Where we draw the line

Two things this deliberately will not do

Both are things competing tools sell as features. We think both end badly for you, so they are not options you can turn on.

It will not put a skill on your resume that you do not have

Every generated bullet is traced back to something in your own history. If it cannot be traced, you see a warning instead of a polished lie. You are the one who has to sit in the interview.

It will not auto-apply to hundreds of jobs for you

Mass auto-apply is how you get filtered out and, on some sites, banned. You press submit, on jobs you chose, with an application worth reading.

Questions

Where do the jobs come from?

Straight from the companies' own careers pages — the same feeds their site uses. That means no reposted listings, no ghost jobs from an aggregator, and a live link you apply through directly. We only keep the roles based in India.

What is a credit?

One piece of AI work. Scoring one job is one credit; rewriting a resume is one; interview prep is one. Searching for openings is free and never uses any. Free gives you 40 a month, Pro 800 — which in practice is far more than anyone applying seriously gets through.

Do I need an OpenAI key or any other account?

No. A Google sign-in is the only account you need. The AI runs on our side and is included in the plan.

Who can see my resume?

Only you. It is stored against your account and used only to answer your own requests. It is never shown to other users, never sent to employers, and never shown in the operator's own dashboard — that view is built to show counts and revenue only. See the privacy policy.

I am not in tech. Is this useful?

Right now it is built for technology roles — support and operations, cloud and SRE, development, data, QA, security, networking and IT. If your work is one of those, including if you are trying to move from one to another, it will work well. Outside them, the job coverage is not there yet.

Can I cancel?

There is nothing to cancel. Pro is bought a month at a time and simply stops — no auto-debit, no subscription to hunt for. Buy another month when you want one. Refund policy.
