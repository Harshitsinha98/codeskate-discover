Billing questions

Will I be charged automatically next month?

No. There is no mandate and no auto-debit. You pay for a month, it runs out, and nothing happens unless you buy another one. Buying extra months while one is active adds to the end date rather than replacing it.

What happens to my data when Pro runs out?

Nothing is deleted. Your profile, matches, saved jobs and everything already generated stay exactly where they are — you drop back to the free credit allowance and the Pro-only actions stop until you buy another month.

Do unused credits roll over?

No — the allowance resets on the 1st of each month. It is sized so that running out should not happen during a normal search; if it does, tell us, because that is useful information about the sizing.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, within 7 days if you have used fewer than 50 credits — the full policy is on the refunds page. The honest advice is to use the free plan first: it exists so you can judge the match quality before paying anything.
