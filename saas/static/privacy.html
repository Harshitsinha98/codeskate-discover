Privacy

Last updated: 30 August 2026

What is stored

  • Your email and a password hash. The password itself is never stored — only a scrypt hash, which cannot be reversed.
  • The documents you upload. Resumes and brag documents are converted to text on upload and stored as text.
  • Your API key, encrypted. Stored using authenticated encryption under a server-held secret. It is decrypted only in memory, only to make a call you asked for.
  • What the agents produce for you — skill graph, match scores, tailored resumes, outreach drafts, interview briefs, pipeline stages.
  • Usage records — which agent ran, which model, token counts and cost.

Who can see your documents

You can. The operator's admin view shows counts and status only — how many documents you uploaded, whether a skill graph exists, how many jobs you scored. It does not display your documents, your skill graph, or anything the agents wrote for you, and it cannot decrypt your API key. It shows only the provider name and the last four characters.

This is enforced in the code, not only in this policy. It is a deliberate limit: it reduces what a compromised operator account could expose.

Where your data goes

To run an agent, the relevant part of your profile and a job description are sent to the AI provider you chose — OpenAI or Anthropic — using your own API key. That request is governed by your agreement with that provider, not by this service. Nothing is sent to any other third party, and your data is never sold, shared with recruiters, or used to train models by us.

Job postings are fetched from companies' own public job boards. No credentials of yours are involved and nothing about you is sent to them.

Shared versus private

Job postings and company briefings are shared across all accounts, because they are identical for everyone and fetching them once keeps costs flat. Nothing derived from you is shared: your scores, resumes, pipeline and spend are visible only to your account.

Deleting things

  • Remove an uploaded document from the dashboard at any time.
  • Remove your stored API key from Settings at any time.
  • To delete your account and everything attached to it, email the operator. Deletion cascades — documents, profile, scores, artifacts and usage records all go.

Retention

Your data is kept while your account exists. Sessions expire after 30 days. Password reset links expire after 60 minutes and work once.

Honest limitations

This is an early-stage product run by an individual, not a company with a security team. Encryption, hashing and tenant isolation are implemented and tested, but no independent security audit has been done. Use a spend limit, and use an API key scoped to this purpose so you can revoke it without affecting anything else.

Contact

Questions, or an account deletion request: contact the operator at the address shown on the sign-in page.
