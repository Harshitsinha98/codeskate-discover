What this service does

CodeSkate reads your career documents and public job postings, scores how well you fit each role, and helps you prepare the application. It is a tool. It does not find you a job, does not apply on your behalf, and makes no promise about any outcome.

Accounts

Sign-in is through Google; there is no password for you to manage. You must be at least 18 and using your own Google account. An account may be disabled if it is used in a way that breaks these terms or that puts the service at risk for other users. You can request deletion at any time.

Plans, credits and payment

The free plan includes a monthly allowance of credits. Pro costs ₹999 per month and is bought one month at a time through Razorpay. There is no auto-debit and no recurring mandate: a Pro month simply ends, and nothing is charged again unless you choose to buy another. Buying while a month is still running extends the end date rather than replacing it.

One credit is one AI action. Searching for job openings does not use credits. The monthly allowance resets on the 1st and unused credits do not carry over. We may change prices or allowances for future purchases, but never for a month you have already paid for.

Refunds are covered by the refund policy, which forms part of these terms.

Fair use

Credits are per person, for your own job search. Sharing an account, reselling access, or running automated traffic against the service are all grounds for the account being disabled. A service-wide daily ceiling also exists as a safety measure; if it is reached, actions are refused temporarily for everyone rather than being billed.

Accuracy, and the part you must check

AI output can be wrong. The resume feature is constrained to facts you supplied and its output is programmatically checked for invented claims — but you are responsible for everything you send to an employer. Read every generated resume and message before it goes out.

Match scores are estimates of how a recruiter is likely to react, not a prediction. Salary bands are extrapolations, labelled with a confidence level, and are not market data. Company briefings may be out of date. Verify anything time-sensitive before you rely on it in an interview or a negotiation.

  • Use the service to misrepresent your experience or qualifications to an employer.
  • Attempt to reach another account's data, or to probe, scrape or overload the service.
  • Use it to send bulk unsolicited messages. It exists to help you apply well, not to apply everywhere.
