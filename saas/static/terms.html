What this service does

CodeSkate runs a set of AI agents over your own career documents and public job postings to help you find and apply for roles. It is a tool. It does not find you a job, and it makes no promise about outcomes.

Your API key and costs

You supply your own OpenAI or Anthropic key, so model usage is billed to your account by that provider. A per-account spend limit is enforced before every call, but you are responsible for the charges on your key. Set the limit low and check your provider's dashboard.

Accuracy, and the part you must check

AI output can be wrong. The resume agent is constrained to facts you supplied and its output is programmatically checked for invented content, but you are responsible for everything you send to an employer. Read every tailored resume before submitting it.

Compensation estimates are low-confidence extrapolations, not market data, and are labelled as such. Company briefings may be out of date. Verify anything time-sensitive yourself before relying on it in an interview.

What you must not do

  • Upload documents or details belonging to someone else without their consent.
  • Use the service to misrepresent your experience or qualifications.
  • Attempt to access another account's data, or to probe or overload the service.
  • Automate bulk applications through the service. It is built to help you apply well, not to blast applications.
